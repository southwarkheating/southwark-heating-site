Boiler replacement

Replacing an old or failing boiler, including a fixed-price like-for-like combi-to-combi offer.

Request a Quote Call Now: 07988 991963

When replacing makes sense

Is your boiler becoming unreliable, with repair bills adding up? There can come a point where replacing it is the more sensible choice. If you are unsure, tell us what is happening and we will advise on the next step, whether that is a repair or a replacement.

Our fixed-price offer

If you have a combi boiler and want a straightforward like-for-like replacement, our offer is the Ideal Logic Max C24 combi boiler for £2,300 fully installed, subject to a site assessment.

  • Professional installation by a Gas Safe registered engineer
  • Boiler commissioned and tested
  • Old boiler removed

Full details and terms are on the boiler replacement offer page.

Please note

The £2,300 price applies only to a like-for-like combi-to-combi replacement. Replacing a different type of boiler, or moving the boiler to a new location, is not like-for-like and is quoted separately. The offer is subject to a site assessment.

Other boiler replacements

If your boiler is not a combi, or you want something different, we assess the property and give you a quote. See boiler installation. If you are not sure whether your boiler is a combi, send us the make and model if you know it and we will contact you to discuss suitable options.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a boiler replacement cost?

Our fixed-price offer is £2,300 fully installed for a like-for-like combi-to-combi replacement with the Ideal Logic Max C24, subject to a site assessment. Other boiler replacements are quoted after we have assessed the property.

What is a like-for-like combi-to-combi replacement?

We remove your existing combi boiler and fit the Ideal Logic Max C24 in its place. It does not cover every possible boiler installation.

Is any extra work included in the £2,300?

Standard installation is included. Any required alterations to pipework, flue, condensate, controls, electrical work or other additional work are identified and quoted separately where applicable.

Who carries out the installation?

Gas work is carried out by a Gas Safe registered engineer (registration No. 985690). You can check any engineer on the Gas Safe Register before work begins.
