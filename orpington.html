Heating, boiler and plumbing help in Orpington

Orpington is mostly family houses from the 1930s onwards. Many have a combi, and plenty still run a system or regular boiler with a cylinder.

Orpington is in the London Borough of Bromley, close to the Kent border. Priory Gardens, beside the medieval Orpington Priory, is just off the high street.
