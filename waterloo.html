Heating, boiler and plumbing help in Waterloo

Homes around Waterloo are mostly flats, from Victorian terraces split into flats and mansion blocks to modern apartments. We look after boilers, heating and plumbing in all of them, and carry out CP12 certificates for the many rented flats.

Waterloo straddles two boroughs: Waterloo station and most of the area are in the London Borough of Lambeth, while the streets to the east run into the London Borough of Southwark. As well as the station, the area is home to The Old Vic theatre and the restaurants along Lower Marsh and The Cut.

Flats and conversions

Many flats here have a compact combi boiler in a kitchen or airing cupboard. We service, repair and replace them, and check the flue and ventilation every time.

Landlords and letting agents

We carry out CP12 landlord gas safety certificates and can combine the check with a boiler service on the same visit, so one appointment covers both.

Newer apartments

Newer flats usually have a modern combi or system boiler. Servicing it every year keeps the manufacturer’s warranty valid and helps it run efficiently.
