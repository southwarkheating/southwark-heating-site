How the plan works

  • 12-month term, paid monthly. Your plan runs for 12 months from the start date on your welcome letter. After that it continues month to month and you can cancel with one month’s notice.
  • Free repair visits start after 30 days. They can be used from 30 days after your start date. Your annual service can be booked straight away.
  • Free repair visits cover labour only. Parts are always quoted and agreed with you before any work starts, and your parts discount is taken off.
  • Recalls don’t use up a visit. If we need to come back within 30 days for the same fault we attended on a free repair visit, that recall is free and doesn’t count. A different, unrelated fault counts as a new visit.
  • Priority appointments. Silver and Gold members are offered the first available appointment within 24 working hours (Monday to Friday, 8am to 6pm). If we can’t, you get the next available slot and any priority fee is refunded. Delays outside our control, such as illness or severe weather, don’t count.
  • Our promise to you. If, through our fault, we don’t carry out your annual service within 13 months, or we miss a priority slot twice in 12 months, you can cancel the rest of your contract with no charge beyond the services you’ve already had.
  • 14 days to change your mind. You have the legal right to cancel within 14 days of joining, without giving a reason, for a full refund. If you ask us to carry out a service or repair during those 14 days, we deduct the normal price of that work.
  • Not insurance. This is a service and maintenance plan, not an insurance policy. It doesn’t cover the cost of a replacement boiler or unlimited repairs.

Common questions about the Care Plan

Is my boiler eligible?

Most gas boilers in good working order are. We check every new sign-up. If your boiler isn’t suitable, we’ll cancel your plan within 14 days and refund you in full. If it has a fault when you join, fixing that fault is a normal paid repair and isn’t covered by your free visits.

What counts as a free repair visit?

The labour for one repair visit, up to £90, which is our standard first-hour charge. Parts are extra, and always quoted and agreed before work starts.

Do unused free visits roll over?

No. Free repair visits reset at the start of each plan year.

Can I cancel?

Yes. You can cancel within 14 days of joining for a full refund, or at the end of your 12-month term. If you leave early you only pay for the services you’ve actually had, at our normal prices, less what you’ve paid. There is no exit fee.

Does the plan include a CP12 gas safety certificate?

Not as standard. Landlords can add a CP12 at our usual price of £90, or £160 for a CP12 plus boiler service.

How do I book once I’m a member?

Call 07988 991963 or log in to your customer account. We’ll apply your free visits and discounts for you.
