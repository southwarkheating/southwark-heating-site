Heating, boiler and plumbing help in Shooters Hill

Shooters Hill has a mix of houses and flats. Homes on higher ground can be exposed to the weather, so condensate pipes and flues are worth checking before winter.

Shooters Hill is in the Royal Borough of Greenwich, near the border with Bexley. Oxleas Wood and Severndroog Castle are on the hill, one of the highest points in South London.
