Heating, boiler and plumbing help in Woolwich

Woolwich has new riverside apartments as well as Victorian terraces and estates. We look after combi and system boilers, and carry out CP12 certificates for rented homes.

Woolwich is in the Royal Borough of Greenwich. The Royal Arsenal riverside, once a huge munitions works, is now homes, shops and riverside walks.

Newer apartments

Newer flats usually have a modern combi or system boiler. Servicing it every year keeps the manufacturer’s warranty valid and helps it run efficiently.

Landlords and letting agents

We carry out CP12 landlord gas safety certificates and can combine the check with a boiler service on the same visit, so one appointment covers both.

Older houses

Older homes often have ageing pipework, radiators and valves. We fix leaks, replace radiators and valves, and can power-flush a sluggish heating system.
