Pest Control Services

Professional pest control for common domestic pest problems, from the same South London business you already trust with your heating and gas.

Make a Pest Control Enquiry Call Now: 07988 991963

Common household pests we can help with

Pests can be unpleasant, and in some cases a risk to health or property. We provide pest control services for common domestic pest problems across South London and surrounding areas.

Mice

Help with mice in the home, including droppings, noises in walls or ceilings and signs of gnawing.

Rats

Help with rats in houses, lofts, gardens and outbuildings.

Cockroaches

Help with cockroaches in kitchens, bathrooms and other warm areas of the home.

Wasps

Help with wasp nests and wasps around your home.

Ants

Help with ants indoors and around the property.

Fleas

Help with flea problems in homes, including those linked to pets.

Bed bugs

Help with suspected or confirmed bed bug problems.

Other household pests

Not sure what you are dealing with, or have another common pest? Contact us to discuss it.

How it works

  • Tell us the problem. Describe what you have seen, where and for how long.
  • We discuss your options. We will talk through the situation and the next steps with you.
  • We agree the work and price. Details and costs are agreed with you before any work starts.

Pest control is offered in addition to our Gas Safe registered heating, plumbing and gas services. Our Gas Safe registration (No. 985690) relates to our gas work.

Professional standards, training and qualifications

Our pest control services are carried out to the appropriate professional standards. For every job, the work is undertaken by personnel who hold the relevant training, qualifications and certifications required for the work being carried out.

We take a careful, responsible approach on every visit, with the safety of you, your family, your pets and your property in mind. If you have any questions about the work being carried out, or about the training and qualifications behind it, please get in touch and we will be happy to discuss them with you.
