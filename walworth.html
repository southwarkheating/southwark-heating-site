Heating, boiler and plumbing help in Walworth

Walworth mixes Georgian and Victorian terraces, large estates and the new developments around Elephant and Castle. Some new blocks are on a heat network, while older homes usually have a combi boiler.

Walworth is in the London Borough of Southwark, south of Elephant and Castle. East Street Market, one of the area’s longest-running street markets, is just off Walworth Road.

Flats and conversions

Many flats here have a compact combi boiler in a kitchen or airing cupboard. We service, repair and replace them, and check the flue and ventilation every time.

Blocks with shared heating

If your block is on a heat network or communal boiler, the main plant belongs to the building. Tell us when you book and we will explain what we can work on in your flat, such as radiators and plumbing.

Landlords and letting agents

We carry out CP12 landlord gas safety certificates and can combine the check with a boiler service on the same visit, so one appointment covers both.
