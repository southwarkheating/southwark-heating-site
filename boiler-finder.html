Good To Know

Which Boilers Will I Be Offered?

We use the answers you give about your home, such as bedrooms, bathrooms and radiators, to show boilers with enough heating and hot-water output.

Is The Price Fixed?

The price shown is fixed for the work described. If we find anything on site that needs extra work, we agree the price with you before we start.

What If My Home Needs A Closer Look?

Some homes, such as those with oil or LPG boilers or a very high hot-water demand, need a personal quote. We will tell you, and you can call 07988 991963 or request a quote.

Where Do My Details Go?

Your details are only asked for when you book. Our Privacy Policy explains how we handle personal information.
