Good To Know

How Accurate Is The Recommendation?

It is based on the information you provide. Final boiler suitability, system requirements and installation requirements are confirmed by Southwark Heating following a professional assessment. It is not a substitute for a professional heat-loss calculation where required.

Is The Price Final?

No. Prices shown are estimates based on the information you supplied. The final price may change if the installation needs additional work or materials. We confirm the final recommendation and installation price before any work starts.

What If No Boiler Is Shown?

Some homes need a quick call or survey first. If that happens we will tell you, and you can call us on 07368 268347 or request a quote.

Where Do My Details Go?
