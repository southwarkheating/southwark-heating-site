Heating, boiler and plumbing help in Brockley

Brockley is known for its large Victorian houses, many of them divided into flats. Bigger houses that are still single homes often have a regular boiler and a hot water cylinder.

Brockley is in the London Borough of Lewisham. Hilly Fields park gives some of the best views across South London.

Older houses

Older homes often have ageing pipework, radiators and valves. We fix leaks, replace radiators and valves, and can power-flush a sluggish heating system.

Flats and conversions

Many flats here have a compact combi boiler in a kitchen or airing cupboard. We service, repair and replace them, and check the flue and ventilation every time.

Homes with a hot water cylinder

Larger houses often have a system or regular boiler and a cylinder. Our boiler finder shows like-for-like options, or you can switch to a combi if it suits your home.
