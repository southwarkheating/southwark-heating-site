Heating, boiler and plumbing help in Nunhead

Nunhead is mainly Victorian and Edwardian terraces with a village feel. Many still have older radiators and pipework, so we often combine a boiler service with fixing sticking valves or cold spots.

Nunhead is in the London Borough of Southwark, just south-east of Peckham. Nunhead Cemetery, one of London’s “Magnificent Seven” Victorian cemeteries, is a local landmark.

Older houses

Older homes often have ageing pipework, radiators and valves. We fix leaks, replace radiators and valves, and can power-flush a sluggish heating system.

Family homes

From a boiler that keeps losing pressure to radiators that stay cold at the top, we sort out the heating and plumbing problems that busy family homes run into.

Landlords and letting agents

We carry out CP12 landlord gas safety certificates and can combine the check with a boiler service on the same visit, so one appointment covers both.
