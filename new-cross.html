Heating, boiler and plumbing help in New Cross

With so many shared and rented houses, landlord gas safety certificates and quick repairs are a big part of what we do in New Cross. Most homes are Victorian terraces or flats.

New Cross is in the London Borough of Lewisham, on the border with Southwark. Goldsmiths, University of London is here, so many homes are rented to students and young professionals.

Shared and student houses

Rented homes need a gas safety check every year. We carry out CP12 inspections, keep landlords informed, and fix faults quickly between tenancies.

Landlords and letting agents

We carry out CP12 landlord gas safety certificates and can combine the check with a boiler service on the same visit, so one appointment covers both.

Older houses

Older homes often have ageing pipework, radiators and valves. We fix leaks, replace radiators and valves, and can power-flush a sluggish heating system.
