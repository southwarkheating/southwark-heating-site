Heating, boiler and plumbing help in Catford

Catford has rows of Edwardian and 1930s houses as well as flats around the town centre. Family homes here are a mix of combi, system and regular boilers.

Catford is in the London Borough of Lewisham and is the borough’s civic centre. The Broadway Theatre and the giant cat above the shopping centre are local landmarks.
