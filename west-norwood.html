Heating, boiler and plumbing help in West Norwood

West Norwood is largely Victorian terraces and conversions. Servicing, combi replacements and radiator repairs are the usual jobs.

West Norwood is in the London Borough of Lambeth. West Norwood Cemetery, another of London’s “Magnificent Seven”, is on Norwood Road.

Older houses

Older homes often have ageing pipework, radiators and valves. We fix leaks, replace radiators and valves, and can power-flush a sluggish heating system.

Flats and conversions

Many flats here have a compact combi boiler in a kitchen or airing cupboard. We service, repair and replace them, and check the flue and ventilation every time.

Landlords and letting agents

We carry out CP12 landlord gas safety certificates and can combine the check with a boiler service on the same visit, so one appointment covers both.
