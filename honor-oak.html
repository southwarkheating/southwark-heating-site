Heating, boiler and plumbing help in Honor Oak

Honor Oak is mainly Edwardian houses and conversions. A boiler service each year and the odd radiator or valve repair keep most of these homes running smoothly.

Honor Oak sits on the border of the London Boroughs of Lewisham and Southwark. One Tree Hill, with its views over London, is at the top of the area.

Older houses

Older homes often have ageing pipework, radiators and valves. We fix leaks, replace radiators and valves, and can power-flush a sluggish heating system.

Family homes

From a boiler that keeps losing pressure to radiators that stay cold at the top, we sort out the heating and plumbing problems that busy family homes run into.

Flats and conversions

Many flats here have a compact combi boiler in a kitchen or airing cupboard. We service, repair and replace them, and check the flue and ventilation every time.
