Heating, boiler and plumbing help in Vauxhall

Vauxhall has riverside towers, estates and Victorian terraces. Newer towers are often on a heat network, while flats in older buildings have their own boiler.

Vauxhall is in the London Borough of Lambeth. The new Nine Elms developments to the west are in Wandsworth. Vauxhall Pleasure Gardens, once famous for its 18th-century entertainments, is now a public park.

Newer apartments

Newer flats usually have a modern combi or system boiler. Servicing it every year keeps the manufacturer’s warranty valid and helps it run efficiently.

Blocks with shared heating

If your block is on a heat network or communal boiler, the main plant belongs to the building. Tell us when you book and we will explain what we can work on in your flat, such as radiators and plumbing.

Flats and conversions

Many flats here have a compact combi boiler in a kitchen or airing cupboard. We service, repair and replace them, and check the flue and ventilation every time.
