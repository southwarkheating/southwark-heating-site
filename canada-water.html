Heating, boiler and plumbing help in Canada Water

Homes here range from 1980s Docklands houses to new apartment blocks. Newer flats often have a system boiler or a heat network supply, so it helps to tell us what you have when you book. We are based in SE16, so this is our home patch.

Canada Water is in the London Borough of Southwark, in the old Surrey Docks area of Rotherhithe. The area is named after the Canada Water dock, which is now a lake beside the library and shops.

Newer apartments

Newer flats usually have a modern combi or system boiler. Servicing it every year keeps the manufacturer’s warranty valid and helps it run efficiently.

Blocks with shared heating

If your block is on a heat network or communal boiler, the main plant belongs to the building. Tell us when you book and we will explain what we can work on in your flat, such as radiators and plumbing.

Family homes

From a boiler that keeps losing pressure to radiators that stay cold at the top, we sort out the heating and plumbing problems that busy family homes run into.
