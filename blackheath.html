Heating, boiler and plumbing help in Blackheath

Blackheath has many Georgian and Victorian houses, some very large. These often have system or regular boilers with a cylinder, and sometimes listed-building rules about where a flue can go.

Blackheath is split between the Royal Borough of Greenwich and the London Borough of Lewisham. The open heath itself and the shops of Blackheath Village give the area its character.

Homes with a hot water cylinder

Larger houses often have a system or regular boiler and a cylinder. Our boiler finder shows like-for-like options, or you can switch to a combi if it suits your home.

Older houses

Older homes often have ageing pipework, radiators and valves. We fix leaks, replace radiators and valves, and can power-flush a sluggish heating system.

Family homes

From a boiler that keeps losing pressure to radiators that stay cold at the top, we sort out the heating and plumbing problems that busy family homes run into.
