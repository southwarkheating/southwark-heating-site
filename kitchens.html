Kitchen plumbing done properly

Your kitchen is one of the hardest-working rooms in the house, and the plumbing behind it needs to be right. A badly fitted waste or a loose connection under the sink can go unnoticed for weeks and quietly damage units, flooring and the ceiling below.

Southwark Heating looks after kitchen plumbing for homeowners, landlords and letting agents across South London and surrounding areas. Whether you are replacing a tap, plumbing in a new washing machine or fitting out a whole new kitchen, every connection is made carefully, tested before we leave, and left tidy behind your units.

What we can help with

Kitchen Plumbing

Supply and waste pipework for kitchens of every size, from a single new tap to the plumbing for a complete new kitchen.

Sinks

Fitting of new kitchen sinks, including single and double bowls, wastes, traps and overflows, all sealed and tested for leaks.

Taps

Replacement of worn or dripping taps and fitting of new mixer taps, with isolation valves so they can be serviced easily in future.

Dishwasher Connections

Plumbing in dishwashers to the water supply and waste, including new supply points where there is not one already.

Washing Machine Connections

Connecting washing machines with hot or cold feeds as required, a proper waste connection and an isolation valve.

Appliance Plumbing

Water connections for fridge freezers with water dispensers, boiling water taps and other kitchen appliances.

Radiator Relocation

Moving radiators to suit a new kitchen layout, with the system drained, refilled and bled so your heating keeps working properly.

Kitchen Plumbing Alterations

Rerouting supply and waste pipework when a sink or appliance moves, planned so it sits neatly behind your new units.

Fitting a new kitchen?

If you are having a new kitchen fitted, we can take care of all the plumbing so your fitter can concentrate on the units and worktops. We can work directly for you or alongside your kitchen fitter.

Before the units go in: we run new supply and waste pipework to where your sink and appliances will be.
Radiators and pipework: we move any radiator or pipe that is in the way of the new layout.
Once the worktops are fitted: we come back to fit the sink and taps and connect your appliances.
Before we leave: everything is tested for leaks and you are shown where your isolation valves are.

Frequently asked questions

Do you plumb in kitchen sinks and taps?

Yes. We fit single and double bowl sinks, mixer taps and the waste, trap and supply pipework that go with them, and make sure everything is sealed and tested before we leave.

Can you connect a dishwasher or washing machine?

Yes. We connect dishwashers and washing machines to the water supply and waste, with an isolation valve so the appliance can be turned off on its own. If there is no supply point where you want the appliance, we can add one.

Can you move my sink or appliances to a different wall?

Usually, yes. Moving a sink or appliance means extending or rerouting the supply and waste pipework. We look at the route and the drainage fall first, then explain what is involved before giving you a price.

Do you work with kitchen fitters?

Yes. We can do the plumbing before the units go in, then come back to connect the sink, taps and appliances once the worktops are fitted, working around your fitter's schedule.

Can you move a radiator to make room for new units?

Yes. We drain down the system, reroute the pipework, refit the radiator in its new position and refill and bleed the system, so your heating works as it should afterwards.

How much does kitchen plumbing cost?

It depends on the job, so we quote once we know what you would like done. Tell us about it through our contact page or give us a call, and we will give you a clear quotation before any work starts.
