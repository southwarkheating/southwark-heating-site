Why replace instead of repair again?

When an ageing boiler keeps breaking down, the repair bills can quickly add up, and you may still be left wondering when the next fault will appear. If that sounds familiar, replacing the boiler can be the more sensible option: you get a new boiler, installed, commissioned and tested, for one clear price.

This offer is for a like-for-like combi-to-combi boiler replacement only: we remove your existing combi boiler and fit the Ideal Logic Max C24 in its place. It does not cover every possible boiler installation.

Is this offer right for you?

The £2,300 price is for a straightforward like-for-like combi-to-combi replacement only, subject to a site assessment.

It is likely to suit you if your current boiler is a combi and you want it replaced with the Ideal Logic Max C24 in the same place.

It does not apply if you want to replace a different type of boiler or move the boiler to a new location. These are quoted separately.

If you are not sure whether your boiler is a combi, choose “Not sure” on the form below. The offer is then not confirmed, and we will contact you to discuss suitable options and provide a separate quote. You can also read about boiler replacement in general.

Frequently asked questions

What does like-for-like combi-to-combi mean?

We remove your existing combi boiler and fit the Ideal Logic Max C24 in its place. It does not cover every possible boiler installation.

Is any extra work included in the £2,300?

Standard installation is included. Non-standard installation work is not. Any required alterations to pipework, flue, condensate, controls, electrical work or other additional work will be identified and quoted separately where applicable.

What if my boiler is not a combi, or I want it moved?

Replacing a different type of boiler, or moving the boiler to a new location, is not a like-for-like combi-to-combi replacement and will be quoted separately. See boiler installation.
