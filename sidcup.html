Heating, boiler and plumbing help in Sidcup

Sidcup is mainly 1930s family houses and some flats. Plenty still have a cylinder, and many are good candidates for a combi when the old boiler fails.

Sidcup is in the London Borough of Bexley. Lamorbey Park, with its lake and historic house, is in the north of the area.
