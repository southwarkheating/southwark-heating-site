Common questions

How much does a boiler service cost?

A boiler service is a £80 fixed price. Any repairs or parts that turn out to be needed are quoted separately. See the price list.

Do you offer emergency call-outs?

Yes. Call us on 07368 268347, tell us the problem and we will advise on the next steps. Emergency and out-of-hours work may be priced differently.

Is your engineer Gas Safe registered?

Yes. Gas work is carried out by a Gas Safe registered engineer, registration No. 985690. You can check any engineer on the Gas Safe Register before work begins.

Do you work with landlords?

Yes. We carry out CP12 gas safety inspections, with or without a boiler service, which can be booked and paid for online. See landlord gas safety certificates.

Which areas do you cover?

South London and surrounding areas. If you are not sure whether we cover your postcode, get in touch and we will let you know.

Many services have a fixed or starting price on our price list. For other jobs, send an enquiry and we will provide a quotation.
